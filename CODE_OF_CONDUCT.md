# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want the Orchestrator community to be a welcoming, harassment-free space for everyone,
regardless of experience level or background.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent; give and accept feedback graciously.
- Keep discussions on-topic and focused on the product.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Posting secrets, credentials, or other people's data.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or lock any content that violates this code, and may block
repeat offenders. To report a concern privately, use GitHub's
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/uptime-lab/orchestrator-tracker/security/advisories/new)
on this repo, or contact a maintainer directly.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
